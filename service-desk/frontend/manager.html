Generate Schedule

Seats and their hours/headcount come from the Seats Configuration sheet, not hardcoded - add, retire, or change a seat there and Generate picks it up live. Mandatory vs. opportunistic and fill priority (Remote Weekday > Remote Weekend > On Campus) are set per seat on that sheet too. No split shifts - one continuous shift per student per day.

Re-running Generate only fills in what's actually new or changed - a student's existing shift is kept as-is unless something genuinely invalidates it (they left, a new conflicting class/time-off, their seat was retired). Adding one new student mid-semester won't reshuffle everyone else's already-settled schedule.

Students on approved time off for this date are skipped. Re-run once it ends.
